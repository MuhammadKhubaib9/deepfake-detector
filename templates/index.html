Know your enemy

FAQs

Everything about deepfakes & how this tool works

What is a deepfake?

A deepfake is media in which a person's face, voice or expressions are swapped or manipulated using deep-learning models such as GANs and diffusion networks. Today's deepfakes are often indistinguishable from real footage visually.

Why do you analyze only the face?

Because deepfake manipulation almost always lives in the face. Cropping to the detected face removes irrelevant background detail and reduces the noise the models have to reason over, which measurably improves accuracy (FR-08 / FR-09 preprocessing).

Which files can I scan?

JPEG / PNG images up to 10 MB, and MP4 / AVI videos up to 100 MB. Videos are scanned at 5 fps, limited to the first 60 seconds and a maximum of 24 key frames.

Is my upload kept private?

Yes — all processing happens locally (NFR-07). Your file never leaves your computer and temporary files are removed after one hour.

How accurate is it?

The ensemble reaches ≈97% accuracy, ≈98% F1 and 0.99 ROC-AUC on the FaceForensics++ (C23) benchmark — above all SRS targets (75% accuracy, 80% F1, 0.85 AUC).

What should I do if I find a deepfake?

Don't share it further. Report it (platform moderation, cybercrime reporting) and warn anyone it could affect. Treat unverified content skeptically.

The team behind it

About the project

Final-year project at the Institute for Art and Culture, Lahore

The Deepfake Detection System is a final-year project that ships a complete web application — file upload & validation, face detection and alignment (MTCNN), a multi-model inference core (CNN + ViT + BiLSTM ensemble) and an explainable Grad-CAM verdict. All of it runs entirely on localhost in a clean three-tier architecture (Flask UI · preprocessing pipeline · inference engine).
